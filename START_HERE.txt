BLUE WHALE PRO – NSE

1) Upload app.py, requirements.txt and README.md to the new GitHub repository.
2) In Streamlit Community Cloud, create a new app from that repository. Main file: app.py.
3) In Streamlit app Settings → Secrets, add:

APP_PASSWORD = "choose-a-private-password"
UPSTOX_ACCESS_TOKEN = "paste-your-token-here"

4) Save secrets and wait for the app to restart.

Do not send your Upstox token to anyone or put it in GitHub.
